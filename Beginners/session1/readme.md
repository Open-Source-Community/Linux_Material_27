🐧 Session 1: Introduction to Linux

Welcome to the first session of the Linux Committee – OSC!

In this session, we take our first steps into the world of Linux. We explore how Linux started, the history behind it, the GNU Project, and how different Linux distributions came to life.

[Session slides] (./session1.pdf)

📚 Topics Covered
History of Linux: Learn about the origins of Linux, how Linus Torvalds started the project in 1991, and how it evolved into one of the most widely used operating system kernels.
The GNU Project: Understand the GNU Project, its foundation by Richard Stallman, its philosophy of free software, and its role in building a complete Unix-like operating system.
GNU/Linux: Discover the relationship between GNU and the Linux kernel and why the combination is commonly referred to as GNU/Linux.
Linux Distributions (Distros): Learn what Linux distributions are, why they exist, and how they differ from one another.
Popular Linux Distros: Get introduced to some well-known distributions, such as:
Ubuntu
Debian
Fedora
Arch Linux
Linux Mint
Kali Linux
🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

Understand the history and origins of Linux.
Explain the difference between Linux and GNU/Linux.
Understand the importance of the GNU Project and free software.
Know what Linux distributions are and why there are so many.
Recognize popular Linux distributions and their use cases.
🚀 Let's Get Started!

Linux is more than just an operating system; it's an ecosystem built around collaboration, freedom, and open-source development.

Let's explore its history and understand the foundations before diving into the terminal in our next session!
